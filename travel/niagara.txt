📅 Day 1: The Western Push (Art & Grand Canyons)

7:30 AM: Depart Union City / Jersey City, NJ (approx. 4-hour drive).

11:30 AM – 2:30 PM | Corning Museum of Glass: 
Explore the galleries, catch a live glass-blowing demonstration, and grab a quick lunch.

3:30 PM – 5:30 PM | Letchworth State Park: 
Drive to the 6657 Middle Fls Rd parking lot. View the massive Middle and Upper Falls, and check out the nearby Humphrey Nature Center area.

7:30 PM | Arrive at Niagara Falls: Check into your hotel, grab dinner, and walk out to see the famous Night Illumination Show.

📅 Day 2: Mighty Falls & Cayuga Lake Scenic Drive

9:00 AM – 1:30 PM | Niagara Falls Experience: Spend the morning exploring the park, riding the Maid of the Mist boat tour, or standing on the Hurricane Deck at Cave of the Winds.

1:30 PM – 4:15 PM: Depart Niagara Falls and drive east toward the Finger Lakes.

4:15 PM – 4:45 PM | Taughannock Falls Overlook (Cayuga Lake): Skip the long lower gorge walk. Instead, drive straight to the roadside upper overlook for a fast, panoramic view of the 215-foot waterfall cutting through the canyon.

4:45 PM – 5:30 PM | Cayuga Lake Scenic Byway: Take a beautiful, relaxed drive north along Route 89 directly alongside the water.

5:30 PM | Arrive in Auburn, NY: Check into your Night 2 hotel early, unwind, and head out for a relaxed 6:30 PM or 7:00 PM dinner.

📅 Day 3: The Lake Paddle, Magical Gorge & Return

8:15 AM: Check out of your Auburn hotel early to beat the crowds.

9:00 AM – 10:30 AM | Seneca Lake Kayak: Arrive at the southern tip of Seneca Lake (686 E 4th St). 

10:45 AM – 1:15 PM | Watkins Glen State Park: Drive 2 minutes down the road to the main lot. Hike the iconic 1.5-mile Gorge Trail to walk under and behind 19 distinct waterfalls.

1:15 PM – 2:00 PM: Grab a quick lunch in downtown Watkins Glen.

2:00 PM – 6:00 PM: Complete the smooth 4-hour drive back to NJ, arriving home completely unpacked and relaxed before late-night exhaustion sets in.

💡 3 Golden Rules for Success Reuse Your Parking Pass: The $10 vehicle entry fee you pay at any
 New York State Park (Letchworth, Taughannock, or Watkins Glen) is valid at all other state parks for that entire calendar day. 

 Keep your dashboard receipt!

 Keep to the 2:30 PM Corning Exit: Watch the clock at the glass museum on Day 1 
 so you don't push your Letchworth waterfall visit into total darkness.

 Download Offline Maps: Cell service can get patchy in the deep valleys around Letchworth and Watkins Glen. Pre-download your Google Maps areas for offline use before you leave home.
